Appliance loads

Alternative to named load profiles: house demand is the sum of appliances
advanced on a fixed sim-time tick (Step), not a next-event queue.

Goal
- Pluggable ApplianceBehaviour strategies (fridge, always-on, HVAC, …).
- ApplianceLoadSystem steps every IntervalMs; each behaviour gets dt.
- HouseLoad.Source selects Profile vs Appliances; live P/Q still feed wiring/loadflow.
- Global outdoor temperature: diurnal sine (mean 20 °C ± 5 °C, coldest ~05:00)
  plus slowly varying noise.
- HVAC: setpoint 20 °C, on when |Tindoor-20| > 1 °C. Indoor leaks toward outdoor
  proportional to ΔT (colder days sap faster); HVAC adds a fixed heat/cool drive.

Acceptance
- Residential kit at spawn: fridge + always_on + HVAC.
- ApplianceLoadSystem advances ambient + steps appliances; updates HouseLoad/PQ.
- LoadTickSystem only updates DemandProfile houses.
- Behaviours register by kind; new kinds need no system changes.
